## About
Con Cariño, "with love" in Spanish, is a self-hosted app for coordinating the care of one loved one alongside the household finances that surround it. It keeps the shared family ledger, the caregiver coverage calendar, invoices for paid caregivers, and the document library in one place.

I was inspired to develop this app after my dad gave me a tour of a spreadsheet that he crafted to share with my family for managing the care of my grandma. While the spreadsheets were impressive, with 5 users and increasingly complicated data being managed the limitations of a shared spreadsheet where being tested. 

## Notable Features

**Family Ledger**

Accounts — checking, credit card, cash, etc: organized into groups, each either shared with the family or kept private
Transactions covering expenses, income, transfers, balance adjustments, refunds, and reimbursements
Reconciliation mode for working an account against a statement, tracking each row as uncleared, cleared, needs review, or reconciled, with an audit trail of who changed what
Payees, categories, and tags 
Attachments
