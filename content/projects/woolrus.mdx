## About

Woolrus allows a company using WooCommerce to send orders to their packing warehouse by using a fulfillment status in WordPress. Then, warehouse staff can pick all the items in the order, package the items into boxes (that are predefined for speed and what is available), and also raise any quality or out of stock alerts. Then the orders can be verified by other users in a quality dashboard. 

Having data on every facet of the order such as what boxes contain which items, predetermined box dimensions, weight, etc, saved a great deal of hours of manual entry for logistics staff. Woolrus additionally programmatically selects the cheapest shipping options available (whether it be a different shipping method or differing negociated rates from the same method) saving on average 25 to 35% on freight cost on average.
